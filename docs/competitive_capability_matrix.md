## 1. Evaluation Scope & Policy Statement

In accordance with Phase 32 Competitor Claim Policy:
- **No Unsubstantiated Parity Claims**: ECDAT does not claim blanket "IBM parity", "SandboxAQ parity", "surpasses IBM", "better than IBM", or "10/10 against IBM".
- **Factual Comparison Basis**: All comparison points are grounded in publicly available vendor technical whitepapers, product manuals, official conference presentations, and published open standards (e.g. OWASP CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM specification co-authored by IBM Research).
- **Transparency on Limitations**: Every capability comparison explicitly documents the limitations of the comparison, acknowledging differences in execution context (e.g., open cloud-native Linux environments vs. proprietary mainframe z/OS hardware).

## 3. Key Findings & Technical Distinction Summary

1. **Open Specifications vs. Closed Ecosystems**:
   - ECDAT focuses exclusively on open standards: OWASP CycloneDX 1.6 CBOM, SPDX 2.3, NIST FIPS 203/204/205, RFC 5424, and standardized Tree-sitter AST grammars.
   - IBM features deep specialized integrations with proprietary hardware (IBM Z mainframe Crypto Express coprocessors, RACF, and z/OS) which are intentionally out of scope for ECDAT.
   - SandboxAQ provides commercial multi-tenant SaaS dashboards and managed agents, whereas ECDAT provides a self-contained, auditable, developer-first platform.

2. **Ground Truth & Evidence Integrity**:
   - ECDAT enforces an explicit anti-deception rule: no capability is reported as verified without physical source code, automated unit tests, an executable demo command, committed documentation, and empirical evidence in the repository.
   - Metric claims are verified against documented baselines, avoiding subjective marketing phrases like "better than" or "surpasses".

3. **Runtime Observation Grounding**:
   - eBPF runtime observation is truthfully documented as `experimentally implemented` (functional on Linux kernel >= 5.8 with `CAP_BPF`, with transparent mock fallback on macOS and Windows).
